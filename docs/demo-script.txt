CREATORAI DEMO SCRIPT

HOOK

You're probably spending hours editing videos that AI could edit in minutes.

BODY

Creating short-form content sounds simple, but creators spend hours watching recordings, finding the best moments, trimming clips, adding captions and preparing content for different platforms.

CreatorAI changes that.

Upload one recording and its script.

CreatorAI analyzes the content, identifies strong hooks, discovers high-potential moments and recommends the best clips.

The creator can then review AI suggestions, choose a clip and customize captions, audio and aspect ratio inside the editor.

Once everything looks right, CreatorAI sends the production plan to the video engine, where FFmpeg creates the final video.

The result is platform-ready content without hours of repetitive editing.

ENDING

One recording.

Multiple platforms.

Minutes instead of hours.

That's CreatorAI.
