# CreatorAI — Judge Questions

## 1. What problem does CreatorAI solve?
Creators spend hours manually finding good moments, trimming videos, adding captions, resizing content and preparing videos for multiple platforms. CreatorAI reduces this repetitive production work.

## 2. Where is AI used?
AI analyzes the creator's content, identifies hooks and important moments, ranks potential clips and generates editing suggestions.

## 3. What makes CreatorAI different?
CreatorAI combines content intelligence with an editable production workflow. AI recommends what should be edited, but the creator remains in control.

## 4. What does FFmpeg do?
FFmpeg performs the actual video processing including trimming, concatenation, resizing, captions, audio processing, background music and final rendering.

## 5. Why separate AI and FFmpeg?
AI decides WHAT content is valuable. FFmpeg performs HOW the final video is produced.

## 6. Can users override AI?
Yes. AI suggestions can be reviewed, ignored or modified before rendering.

## 7. What is Creator Intelligence?
Creator Intelligence converts AI analysis into useful metrics including:
- Top Hooks
- Best Clips
- Average Engagement
- Content Patterns
- Production Time Saved

## 8. What formats are supported?
CreatorAI supports:
- 9:16 — Reels / Shorts
- 16:9 — YouTube
- 1:1 — Square social content

## 9. What happens if AI gives a bad suggestion?
The creator can reject the suggestion and manually modify the production plan.

## 10. Why not completely automate editing?
Creative decisions are subjective. CreatorAI acts as an intelligent production assistant while preserving human creative control.

## 11. What is your main innovation?
Our main innovation is connecting AI content intelligence, editable recommendations and automated video rendering in one creator workflow.

## 12. What is the complete workflow?
Idea → Script → Recorded → AI Analysis → Best Clips → Edit → Render → Ready to Publish

## 13. How is your system divided?
- React handles the Creator Workspace.
- FastAPI handles backend communication and AI.
- AI analyzes and ranks content.
- FFmpeg performs video processing.
- Creator Intelligence provides analytics and insights.

## 14. How could CreatorAI scale?
AI analysis and video rendering are separated, allowing compute-heavy video processing and AI workloads to scale independently.

## 15. Who would use CreatorAI?
Content creators, influencers, marketing teams, podcasters, educators and businesses producing short-form video content.
